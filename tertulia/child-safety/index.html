Tertulia child safety standards

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Developer: SD Projects · Contact: sdprojects1985@gmail.com

Tertulia is an independent Spanish-language chat app (not affiliated with Chat Hispano / IRC-Hispano), for people aged 18 and over only.

1. Zero tolerance

We prohibit any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM), and any contact with minors for sexual purposes or grooming. Anyone who tries is not welcome in the app or, under the network's rules, in its rooms.

2. Adults only

The app is not directed at minors. Before entering you must confirm you are 18 or older and accept the rules; the app does not verify age, it is the user's own statement. It is declared on Google Play as adults only.

3. Tools for users

You can ignore or block any user (local and immediate) and report a user: "Report" shows you the exact text of the report before it is used and lets you copy it to paste into the contact form of the chat network, whose operators moderate the rooms (or post it in its help channel, if the network allows it); you send it, with the context you choose to include.

4. How reports are handled

  • Reports about conduct in rooms go to the network's operators, who can kick or ban users.
  • You can also write to the developer (contact below). We review child-safety reports as a priority.
  • Tertulia hosts no content: messages travel over a third party's network and we run no servers.

5. Cooperation with authorities

We will cooperate with the competent authorities and child-protection bodies, and report CSAM that comes to our attention to the appropriate bodies, as required by law.

6. Contact
