Tertulia
Spanish chat rooms.

Join the rooms of the Chat Hispano IRC network with a nickname. No Tertulia account and no servers of our own: your settings and encrypted private history stay on your phone.

No account

You enter with just a nickname. Tertulia does not ask for your real name, email or phone number.

No servers of our own

We have no servers, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Your data, on your phone

Nickname, favourites, settings and encrypted private history are stored only on your phone.

Adults 18 and over only

Tertulia is only for people aged 18 or over and asks you to confirm your age before you enter. It is not aimed at minors.

Independent app

Tertulia is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Chat Hispano / IRC-Hispano. It only uses its network, which is a third-party service.

What you write is seen by others

Your messages reach the servers of the chat network, which belong to a third party, and are seen by the other people in the room or conversation. The connection is encrypted (TLS) by default.

The app is in Spanish

Tertulia’s menus and screens are in Spanish.
