Tertulia privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026
Controller: SD Projects (independent developer) · Contact: sdprojects1985@gmail.com
App: Tertulia: chat en español (Android, identifier com.hispano.ircchat)

1. What Tertulia is

Tertulia is an independent Spanish-language chat app. It lets you connect, with a nickname, to the Chat Hispano (IRC-Hispano) IRC chat network and talk in rooms, private messages and with your contacts. Tertulia is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Chat Hispano / IRC-Hispano: it only uses their network, which is a third-party service.

Tertulia is for adults aged 18 and over only. It is not directed at minors and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

2. Summary

  • There is no Tertulia account: you enter with a nickname. We do not ask for a real name, email or phone number.
  • We run no servers of our own, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.
  • What you write travels to the chat network's servers (a third party) and is seen by the other people in the room or conversation.
  • The connection is encrypted (TLS) by default.
  • Your local data (nickname, favourites, blocked users, settings and encrypted private history) stays on your phone and is excluded from backups.

3. Data Tertulia does NOT collect

Tertulia does not collect, store on servers of its own or sell: accounts, real name, email, phone number, location, phone contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or analytics and crash data. It contains no advertising or analytics SDK. There is no profiling and no tracking across apps.

4. Data sent to the chat network

To chat, the app sends the following to the chat network's servers, temporarily and only while you are connected:

  • your nickname and the technical username the IRC protocol uses;
  • the messages you write (in rooms and private) and the commands you run (join a room, ignore, etc.);
  • technical connection data such as your IP address, which the servers necessarily see when you connect.

These servers are owned and operated by a third party (Chat Hispano). Tertulia does not control what the network logs or for how long; it may keep technical logs such as your IP address. The network's own rules and policy apply to that processing. What you write in a room is public to the people in it, and others may copy or save it outside our control. Do not write anything you do not want to share.

If you register your nickname on the network (the NickServ service, optional), the password and email you provide for that registration are handled by the network, not by Tertulia. The app may send your NickServ password to the network to identify you, only over an encrypted connection: it never sends it unencrypted.

5. Connection encryption

Tertulia connects with TLS (encryption in transit) by default, so other people on your Wi-Fi cannot read your messages. If the secure server does not answer, the app does not switch to an unencrypted connection on its own: it tells you and continues unencrypted only if you expressly accept. While the connection is not encrypted, the app shows a visible "NO CIFRADO" (not encrypted) indicator and sends no passwords. Even when encrypted, the chat network receives your messages in clear text because it is the recipient of the service.

6. Data stored on your device

Stored only on your phone:

  • nickname, connection preferences and settings (theme, text size, alerts);
  • favourites and your list of blocked or ignored users;
  • the private message history, encrypted with a key protected by the Android Keystore, kept for the retention period you choose in Settings;
  • the last-session state (for example, the room you were in).

Tertulia disables Android backups (cloud and device-to-device) for this data: it does not leave your phone. None of this data is sent to any server of ours.

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to connect to the chat network and detect whether you are online.
  • Notifications: to alert you to private messages, mentions and contacts coming online. You can deny the permission or turn each alert type off in Settings.
  • Foreground service (special use): to keep the connection open with the screen off so alerts can reach you. While it runs, a permanent notification with a Disconnect button is shown; it closes the connection and stops the service.

Tertulia does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

8. Moderation, blocking and reports

Rooms are moderated by the chat network's operators, not by Tertulia. You can ignore or block any user (stored on your phone only) and report a user: "Report" prepares a message for the network's operators (or opens their contact form) with the context you choose to include; you send it, and they handle it under their own rules. Tertulia does not receive those reports.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep none of your data on servers of our own, so there is nothing for us to delete. On your phone you can delete a chat's history or all data from Settings ("Borrar todo"), or uninstall the app, which removes all local data. Data the chat network already received (messages, nickname, technical logs) is kept by the network under its own rules; to ask for its deletion, contact the network's operators (the #Opers_Help room or their contact form, chathispano.com/contactar/).

10. Your rights

Because Tertulia keeps no database of your personal data, we hold no data about you to access, correct or erase. If you believe we process personal data about you, write to sdprojects1985@gmail.com and we will respond as applicable law requires (including the GDPR, if you live in the European Economic Area). You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, aepd.es).

11. Minors

Tertulia is only for people aged 18 or over and asks you to confirm your age before you enter. If you know a minor is using it, tell us so we can act.

12. Changes to this policy

If data handling changes (for example, if ads, purchases or an account are added), we will update this policy and the "last updated" date, and notify you inside the app when the change is significant.
