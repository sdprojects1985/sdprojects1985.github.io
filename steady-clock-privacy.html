Privacy Policy

Steady Clock for Android · Last updated 6 October 2026

SDProjects does not collect, sell or share personal data through Steady Clock. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Offline operation

Steady Clock does not request the Android INTERNET permission. An Android app without that permission cannot make its own direct Internet connections. Its city search uses a database bundled with the app. Android system backup is separate and is described below.

What the app stores, and where

Everything Steady Clock remembers is written to its own private storage area on your device, protected by Android's application sandbox. SDProjects does not receive these records.

  • The clocks you have added, and the name you gave your home clock
  • Your settings — clock format, stopwatch and timer preferences, theme
  • Your saved timer presets
  • The current state of the stopwatch and timer, so they survive being closed

Uninstalling the app removes its local data; Android may retain a system backup and restore backed-up preferences when you reinstall. You can also clear local data at any time from Settings → Apps → Steady Clock → Storage.

Android backup

If you have Android’s own backup feature switched on, your settings and saved clocks may be included in the backup that your device makes to your Google account. That backup belongs to you and is governed by Google’s privacy policy, not by us — we never receive it and cannot access it. You can disable it in your Android backup settings.

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhy the app asks
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To display the running timer and its completion alert. If you decline, timer notifications will not appear in the notification shade.
USE_EXACT_ALARM
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM on Android 12		 So a countdown fires at the right second even if the screen is off or the system has put the app to sleep. This is the core function of a timer.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE		 To keep a running timer alive and visible in your notification shade, and to sound the alert when it finishes.
VIBRATE Haptic feedback on the stopwatch, and vibration when a timer ends. Both can be turned off in Settings.
WAKE_LOCK To keep the device awake while the timer alert is ringing, for up to about five minutes.

Steady Clock does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or any other personal data. The list of cities in the app is a fixed file inside the app itself; searching it never leaves your device.

Children

Steady Clock does not collect personal data from children or other users.
